# Roles and permissions

Generated by `pnpm roles:doc` from `src/lib/auth/permissions.ts`. Do not edit by hand.
Source of truth: `docs/source/roles-system.html`.

Logins identify people; they do not protect data. This map decides what the app shows and allows, not what is on disk.

## Scopes

- `all`: everything in the workspace
- `below`: roles below admin
- `team`: their team
- `own`: their own
- `queue`: into the unassigned queue
- `—`: no access
