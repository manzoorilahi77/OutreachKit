## Correction 1 — Cold reply rate is 1–5%, not 12–20%

Section 09 gave Contacted → Replied as 12–20%, and the funnel line, the weeks 3–6 expectation and the kit footer
(all derived from it) followed. They are recomputed from 1–5% and labelled planning estimates. No other statistic was added.

**Arithmetic.** The other ratios are section 09's, unchanged: Replied → Call booked 35–50%, Call → Proposal 60–75%,
Proposal → Won 30–40%. So Call → Won is 0.60 × 0.30 = 0.18 at the low end and 0.75 × 0.40 = 0.30 at the high end.
Each range multiplies the low ends together and the high ends together.

| Figure | Low | High | Shown as |
|---|---|---|---|
| Replies per 100 contacted | 100 × 1% = 1 | 100 × 5% = 5 | 1–5 |
| Calls per 100 contacted | 1 × 35% = 0.35 | 5 × 50% = 2.5 | 0.4–2.5 |
| Closes per 100 contacted | 0.35 × 0.18 = 0.063 | 2.5 × 0.30 = 0.75 | 0.1–0.8 (one decimal) |
| Replies from 200 contacted (weeks 3–6) | 200 × 1% = 2 | 200 × 5% = 10 | 2–10 |
| Calls from 200 contacted (weeks 3–6) | 2 × 35% = 0.7 | 10 × 50% = 5 | 1–5 |
| Closes a month from about 200 contacted (50 leads a week, four weeks) | 200 × 1% × 35% × 0.18 = 0.126 | 200 × 5% × 50% × 0.30 = 1.5 | 0.1 to 1.5 |

## Correction 2 — The first DM is text only

go in once they reply, or in the email. For the fifteen openers, the DM copy button copies the line only, and a second button,
“Line + link (for reply or email)”, copies the line plus the link.
